OUR COMPANY

From development to market.

Based in the heart of Silicon Valley, California, SiliconEng brings together experienced professionals specializing in analog, power management, SoC, and RF integrated circuits and systems. With more than 100 years of combined industry experience, we support products from initial development through to market.

When customer requirements call for it, we have processes in place to support ITAR-compliant work. Our engineering services include testing, product characterization, demo board development, reliability, AEC-Q100, space, and military qualification, datasheet creation, and sample generation.

To streamline scaling, our turnkey solutions cover assembly, mass volume production, warehousing, and global drop shipment leveraging trusted onshore and offshore partners.

OUR TEAM

About SiliconEng

100+years of combined
team experience

SiliconEng brings together highly skilled professionals specializing in analog, power management, SoC, and RF integrated circuits and systems. We help semiconductor teams improve efficiency across post-silicon development, from the first test plan to high-volume production.

Our services span ATE test development, product characterization, demo board development, reliability and AEC-Q100 qualification, datasheet creation, sample generation, warehousing, and release into onshore and offshore high-volume production.

Practical ATE expertise

Our experience covers Teradyne ETS364, ETS88, and ETS800; Accotest; Microflex; UltraFlex; Advantest 93K; Chroma Test; and legacy ASL1000 systems. We also develop custom test systems using Python to automate instruments over USB and GPIB.

Quality at every stage

We develop test plans, design and build test and bench hardware, and write test programs supported by thorough documentation. Our quality checks include Gauge R&R, spike checks, and software robustness testing, with a focus on quality, performance, and on-time delivery.
