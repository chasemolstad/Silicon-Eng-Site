# SiliconEng white papers — website publish package

Decision (2026-10-25): publish OPEN. Both PDFs are public downloads with no
form gate. A voluntary "notify me of new papers" signup may be added to the
Resources page, but it must not block the downloads.

## Contents

- `whitepaper-01-from-datasheet-to-ate.pdf` — Paper 1, Version 2.7 (14 pp)
  "From Datasheet to ATE: A Systematic Method for Power-IC Test Program
  Development." Worked example: TI TPS7A20 (DQN), datasheet SBVS338H
  (July 2024).
- `whitepaper-02-analog-power-test-pitfalls.pdf` — Paper 2, Version 1.8 (19 pp)
  "Analog and Power IC Test Development: A Field Guide to Pitfalls and Good
  Practice." Worked examples: TI TPS54360 buck and TI INA333 instrumentation
  amplifier.
- `whitepaper-01-summary-page.md` — Paper 1 summary-page copy, aligned to v2.6.
- `whitepaper-02-summary-page.md` — Paper 2 summary-page copy, aligned to v1.7.
